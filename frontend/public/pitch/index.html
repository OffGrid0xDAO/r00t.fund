r00t.fund04 / 13 · INSIGHT
The core idea

We don't fight the extractive
algorithm. We redirect it.

Every token on r00t.fund trades on two pools at once: a public Uniswap v4 pool, and a private, shielded zkAMM.

When a trade moves one pool, the two drift apart — and that gap is an arbitrage. Normally a bot races in and pockets the spread. Instead, our Uniswap v4 hook back-runs that arbitrage itself, in the same transaction, and sends the captured spread straight to a land-regeneration treasury.

Arbitrage becomes regeneration.
The privacy premium funds the land.

r00t.fund06 / 13 · MEV DEFENSE
The MEV blocker

The value a bot would steal is
the value that funds the land.

On a normal public AMM, every swap sits exposed in the mempool — sandwiched, front-run, and back-run by searchers. That extracted value (the LVR LPs bleed to arbitrageurs) leaks out of the ecosystem forever. r00t.fund's two-pool design shuts both doors at once.

Extractive MEV · today

The searcher wins

  • Front-run & sandwich — trades visible in the mempool are picked off before they land.
  • Cross-pool arb — the spread between venues is raced for and pocketed by bots.
  • Value leaves — extracted profit exits to the searcher; nothing returns to LPs or the world.
Regenerative capture · r00t.fund

The land wins

  • Nothing to front-run — the shielded zkAMM hides trade amounts, so private-side swaps can't be sandwiched.
  • Arb internalized — the hook back-runs the cross-pool spread atomically in afterSwap — no mempool gap for a searcher to exploit.
  • Value redirected — the exact LVR a bot would take is swept to the plot's regen treasury.

Private trading, public price integrity — and the MEV that would've fed a bot now plants oak trees.

r00t.fund07 / 13 · PRODUCT
The product

A launchpad — where each token
is half a hectare of real earth.

  • A steward creates a land — uploads real boundary & topography, proves a unique human with World ID, gets an ENS identity, and commits $R00T as seed liquidity.
  • They launch parcel tokens through a Continuous Clearing Auction — a uniform-price fair launch, so backers (not the steward) discover the price.
  • On clear, the auction seeds both pools — the public v4 pool and the shielded zkAMM — and registers the market on the shared hook, in one transaction.
  • People trade. Every swap moves the public price, the pools drift, and the hook fires — routing the spread to that plot's regen fund.

The more a parcel is traded, the more its land is regenerated.

🦄 Uniswap Foundation

RegenArbHook + CCA

A production v4 afterSwap hook doing real in-transaction cross-pool arbitrage via flash accounting. One shared hook serves every parcel through a MarketRegistered registry — nothing hardcoded. A Continuous Clearing Auction seeds both pools on launch.

🌍 World

Proof of humanity

Before a wallet can create a land, the steward proves they're a unique human with World ID (IDKit → orb / device). Sybil-resistant land creation — real people steward real land.

🔑 ENS

Steward identity

Every steward gets a human-readable .eth name + avatar resolved from L1, shown across the app. Every parcel is an ENS subname (oak.r00t.eth) whose records show its price, pools & live regen status.

And the privacy is genuine — the shielded pool uses real zero-knowledge proofs (Circom / Groth16, Poseidon Merkle trees). User trade amounts stay private; the public reserves sync every block.
